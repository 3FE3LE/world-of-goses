## Retired MVP shorthand (historical audit trail)

Earlier drafts used three internal shorthand archetypes (martial,
restorative, tactical-engineering) to explore the design space. They
are **historical notes**, not the current roster, and they must not
override the eight canonical lineages or be exposed as public-facing
classes.

Any future implementation must use the original names, cultures,
visual silhouettes, professional affinities, strengths, vulnerabilities,
and environmental relationships described in the design bible. This
note is the only place the retired shorthand is mentioned.
